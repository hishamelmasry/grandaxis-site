1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Grand Axis for General Supplies ("Grand Axis", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the Shader mobile application ("Shader", "the App", تطبيق «شادر»). This policy is published in accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and applicable Egyptian data protection regulations (Law No. 151 of 2020 on Personal Data Protection).

Shader is a market price platform serving two categories of users: Merchants (B2B) — wholesale traders who access daily produce price bulletins, cost calculators, and digital ledger tools; and Consumers (B2C) — household users seeking local produce price guidance and smart shopping tools.

2. Information We Collect

a) Location Data

With your permission, the App collects coarse device location solely to display the wholesale markets ("shawader") and local price bulletins nearest to you. Location is processed on-device to select the relevant market region; it is not used for continuous tracking, advertising, or profiling. You may deny location permission and manually select your market from the in-app list at any time.

b) Phone Numbers (Merchant Registration)

Merchants who join the market-reporting program or use trader features provide a phone number (typically WhatsApp) for identity, communication, and delivery of program benefits (such as promotional advertising rewards). Phone numbers are never displayed publicly without explicit consent and are used only for App-related communication.

c) Technical & Diagnostic Data

The App collects limited technical diagnostics — crash logs, app version, platform type, and a randomly generated device identifier — to detect and fix malfunctions. This identifier carries no personal identity: it is a locally generated random ID and is not linked to your name, phone number, or IP address.

d) User-Generated Content (Stored Locally)

Merchant ledger entries, saved cost templates, and shopping lists created inside the App are stored locally on your device. If you enable the optional Google Drive backup feature, backups are stored in your own Google Drive account, inaccessible to Grand Axis.

4. How We Use Data & Data Security

  • Displaying nearby markets and their daily price bulletins
  • Operating the merchant reporting and rewards program
  • Diagnosing crashes and improving App stability
  • Responding to support and legal requests

All network communication with our servers is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. Access to production systems is restricted and logged. Merchant data is stored on secured servers within the European Union (Germany). We apply the principle of data minimization: we collect only what the App's features strictly require.

5. Data We Never Do

We never sell personal data to any third party.
We never share your data with advertisers.
We never track your continuous location.
We never read your Google Drive backups.

6. Your Rights

You have the right, at any time, to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; withdraw consent for data processing (including location); and request complete deletion of your account and associated data. Deletion requests can be submitted directly in the App or through our dedicated page:

Account & Data Deletion Page

7. Data Retention

Merchant reporting records and reward histories are retained while the account is active. Upon deletion, personal identifiers are removed immediately; aggregated, non-identifiable price statistics may be retained for service continuity. Accounting-related records, if any, are retained for the statutory period required by Egyptian law (5 years), after which they are destroyed.

8. Children's Privacy

Shader is a trade and utilities application directed at adult merchants and household consumers. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
