Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account & Data

Shader (شادر) gives you full control over your information. You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time through either of the two official methods below. Deletion is free, does not require uninstalling the app, and complies with the Google Play Account Deletion Policy.

1

Delete From Inside the App

Fastest method — takes less than a minute

  1. 1

    Open the Shader app and navigate to the Profile tab (حسابي) from the bottom navigation bar.

  2. 2

    Scroll to the Account section and tap "Delete My Account & Data" (حذف حسابي وبياناتي).

  3. 3

    Review the summary of data that will be deleted, then confirm by tapping "Confirm Deletion".

  4. 4

    Your account and server-side personal data are deleted immediately. A confirmation message appears in the app.

2

Web Deletion Request

For users who can no longer access the app on their device

Enter the phone number registered in the app and submit the request. Your email client will open with a pre-filled deletion request to our support team; simply press send. We process verified requests within 5 business days and confirm completion by reply.

Email client did not open?

If the button above does not open your email application, you can send the request manually:

  1. Compose a new email to contact@ingeniatorai.com
  2. Set the subject to: "Delete Account Request"
  3. Include the registered phone number of the account you wish to delete

Requests are processed within 5 business days and confirmed by reply.

What Gets Deleted & What Is Retained

Data CategoryOn Deletion
Phone number & merchant registration Deleted immediately
Market reports submitted by you Anonymized immediately
Reward points & leaderboard entries Deleted immediately
Device diagnostics & random device ID Deleted within 30 days
Ledger & shopping lists (on-device data) Removed when app is uninstalled
Aggregated anonymous price statistics Retained — non-identifiable
Accounting records (where applicable) Retained 5 years — Egyptian law

"Anonymized" means the link between the record and your identity is permanently destroyed — the record can never be traced back to you. Retained items are required for legal compliance and contain no personal identifiers.

Before you delete — please note

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Your reward points and reporter status will be lost.
  • If you only want to stop location tracking, you can revoke the permission from your device settings without deleting your account.
