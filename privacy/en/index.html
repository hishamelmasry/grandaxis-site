Privacy Policy — Shadr

Daily vegetable & fruit prices from Obour Market, Egypt

Last updated: September 20, 2026

📋 Quick summary

Shadr is a simple app for viewing wholesale/retail vegetable and fruit prices. It does not ask for your phone number, email, or location. Your customer ledger (if you're a trader) is stored only on your own device — no one else, including us, can see it.

📊 Data the app collects

Minimal, anonymous usage data to understand adoption and fix bugs:

  • A random device identifier — generated once on your device, not linked to your name, phone number, or any account.
  • App-open timestamps — to count usage.
  • Crash reports — only the error type, never personal data, so we can fix bugs.

That's everything. No location tracking, no access to your contacts, photos, or messages.

💾 Customer ledger (for traders)

If you use the ledger feature, your customers' data (name, phone, balances, transactions) is stored only on your device — never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data, unless you created your own backup file via "My Account" and saved it elsewhere (WhatsApp, Drive, etc.).

🌐 Price source

Displayed prices come from the official daily wholesale bulletin of Obour Market — public market data, not personal information about anyone.

🔔 Price alerts

If you enable price-change alerts, the app periodically checks public market prices in the background and shows you a local notification — no personal data is transmitted during this check.

🤝 Third-party sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers or marketing companies. There is no ad SDK in the app. The minimal usage data described above is stored only on our own server.

🔒 Data security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted over HTTPS. Your ledger is protected by being stored locally on your device, behind your phone's own lock screen.

👶 Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from users in that age group.

♻️ Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the "last updated" date above and describe the change here.
